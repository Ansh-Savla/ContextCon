# Trust and Compliance ideas with CrustData

Combined ideas from [Sol's reply](sol-reply.md) and [Opus's reply](opus-reply.md).

| Idea | What the agent does with CrustData | Finished deliverable | Additional evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Vendor legitimacy check** | Resolves the company; compares submitted details against its domain, location, leadership, workforce, and public presence | Vendor verification report with matched facts, contradictions, and missing evidence | Official registration records to establish legal existence |
| **2. Business impersonation detector** | Compares the claimed company, submitted domain, and representative against canonical company and professional identities | Case report showing domain, company, and employment mismatches | Domain-control and authorized-signatory evidence where required |
| **3. Shell-company risk triage** | Compares claimed scale and operations with headcount history, employee work histories, hiring, and public activity | Evidence matrix identifying inconsistencies and unresolved questions | Ownership, filings, and transaction records for stronger conclusions |
| **4. Vendor conflict-of-interest checker** | Resolves vendors and declared stakeholders; compares professional histories with an authorized internal roster | Relationship map with dated overlaps and disclosure questions | Internal declarations and ownership records |
| **5. Adverse-information investigator** | Resolves the correct entity, searches for relevant reports, fetches sources, and separates namesakes | Dated investigation dossier with source quality, match confidence, and allegation versus finding distinctions | Authoritative regulator, court, sanctions, or PEP records for those specific checks |
| **6. Trade-credit assessment assistant** | Builds company context from size, funding, workforce trends, hiring, and public business signals | Policy-based credit recommendation with assumptions and evidence gaps | Financial statements, payment history, existing exposure, and an explicit credit policy |
| **7. Continuous vendor review** | Tracks supported company/person field changes and reruns relevant checks when evidence changes | Updated risk dossier explaining what changed and why it matters | Separate scheduled checks for sources outside supported watcher fields |
| **8. Invoice and bank-change request investigator** | Resolves the business behind an invoice; compares sender details and the representative's current employment against the claimed vendor | Investigation report highlighting identity discrepancies and unresolved payment-authority checks | Original vendor records, bank-account ownership evidence, and an authorized verification procedure |
| **9. Founder and executive relationship analysis** | Resolves founders and executives, traces their professional histories, and identifies shared company affiliations and relevant adverse reports | Sourced relationship graph with dated affiliations and patterns requiring investigation | Registry records to verify directorships, ownership, dissolution dates, and legal relationships |

Sparse public data alone does not establish fraud or shell-company status. Professional affiliations do not prove ownership or wrongdoing, and a matching employee profile does not establish payment authority. Keep missing evidence separate from contradictory evidence.
