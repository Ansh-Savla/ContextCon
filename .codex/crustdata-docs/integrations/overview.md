## Building a platform or marketplace?

Crustdata ships as a remote MCP server, so listing us takes a URL and an OAuth handshake, with no SDK to maintain on your side. We are already live in the Claude connector directory, the ChatGPT plugin directory, and the Cursor Marketplace.
