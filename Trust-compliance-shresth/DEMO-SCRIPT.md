# SignalGate: 2-minute Loom demo script

**Goal:** show one product taking unseen requests all the way to finished work, built on the CrustData identity graph, with every claim cited.

## Before you hit record (2 minutes of prep)

1. Run `npm run dev` in `Trust-compliance-shresth/signalgate`, then open http://localhost:3000 in an **incognito window**, which avoids the extension "Issue" badge.
2. Set browser zoom to 100% and the window to about 1440×900. Pick **dark** or **light** theme (sidebar switch) and keep it for the whole video.
3. Make sure these cases appear in **Recent cases**, so each opens instantly:
   - BluePine wire
   - BluePine pitch
   - Wicked Good Cupcakes
   - Zeta
4. Close other tabs. In Loom, choose **Screen + Camera** (or **Screen only**), with the mic on.

## Order and script (target 1:55)

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:12** | Home page | "This is SignalGate, a diligence desk for investors and lenders. You paste a request the way you received it, and agents verify the company and its people in CrustData's identity graph, screen for fraud, and **finish the work**, not just flag it." |
| **0:12–0:35** | Click the **BluePine: closing wire instructions** card, then **Run diligence**. Let the 8-step rail and live activity play. | "Here's an email at closing asking us to wire ₹75 lakh to new bank details. The agents resolve the real company, compare the sender's domain, and check whether this 'CFO' has ever worked there." |
| **0:35–0:45** | Result: **Stop the wire**, Not cleared | "Lookalike domain. The sender isn't at the company. The wire is put on hold and a call-back is prepared. Nothing is sent, and the agent contacts no one." |
| **0:45–1:15** | Recent cases → **BluePine pitch**. Scroll slowly: decision, then **Company at a glance** charts, then **Founders & leadership** (click **Naveen**, the drawer opens), then **Attention & next steps** | "A messy forwarded pitch that even gets the name wrong. CrustData's headcount, traffic and funding data show the reality. One co-founder left in October 2023, the team is 4 people, not 20, and the Shark Tank deal is in the press but not in the funding record. So the decision is **Proceed only with conditions**, and the closing checklist lists the documents needed." |
| **1:15–1:22** | Click **Print IC memo**, show it for 2 seconds, then close it | "And the investment-committee memo is ready to print, with every claim cited." |
| **1:22–1:40** | Recent cases → **Wicked Good Cupcakes** (lender). Show **Decline** and the parent company, then scroll to **Credit case**: grade D and the exposure bars | "Same desk, lender side. A $250K credit line from a business that says it's independent. CrustData shows it was acquired by Hickory Farms and has 2 staff, not 150. Grade D: **Decline**." |
| **1:40–1:48** | Recent cases → **Zeta** | "And when the input is ambiguous (25 companies called Zeta), it refuses to guess." |
| **1:48–1:58** | Scroll a case to **Evidence → CrustData data used**, then click **Help** in the sidebar | "Everything about identity, ownership, people and funding comes from CrustData, every claim links to its source, and the rules, not the LLM, make the decision. SignalGate." |

## If something goes wrong while recording

- **A live run is slow:** open the same case from **Recent cases** instead. It replays instantly at 0 credits.
- **You run over 2:00:** drop the Zeta beat.
